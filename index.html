Seekur
How it works

Your cameras, now paying attention.

Seekur watches your existing store cameras for unusual activity and asks you to confirm before anyone is alerted. Runs on your own computer. Video never leaves the store.

Download
On a phone? Install Seekur on the store's computer first. Then open your dashboard here and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  1. Download the installerClick the button above to get Seekur-Setup.exe.
  2. Run itWhile Seekur is in beta the installer isn't signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info → Run anyway. This happens once.
  3. Click Next → Next → FinishSeekur installs as background services that start with Windows, even before anyone logs in.
  4. Set up in your browserThe dashboard opens automatically. Create your account, let Seekur check the computer, pick the recommended plan, and add your cameras.
Use IP cameras on the store network (RTSP/ONVIF). A webcam plugged into a Windows PC may not be usable from a background service; it's fine for a quick test on Ubuntu.
  1. Open a terminal on the store computerUbuntu 22.04 or 24.04.
  2. Run the installer 
    curl -fsSL /install.sh | sudo bash
    It installs everything, sets Seekur to start automatically after a power cut and prints your dashboard address.
  3. Open the dashboard addressFor example http://192.168.1.50:8080 from any computer or phone on the store network.
  4. Set up in your browserCreate your account, let Seekur check the computer, pick the recommended plan, and add your cameras.
  1. Download the .dmgApple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer), macOS 13+.
  2. Open it and double-click “Install Seekur.pkg”While Seekur is in beta it isn't signed by Apple yet. If macOS says it can't be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”.
  3. Set up in your browserSeekur starts in the background and your browser opens the setup page. Same steps as every other system.
On Mac, Seekur works with IP cameras on the store network. macOS doesn't let background services use a built-in webcam.

1 · It watches

Open-source AI finds people and body posture in your existing cameras, and checks them against rules you control: lingering, restricted areas, after-hours movement, falls, long queues, possible concealment.

2 · You confirm

Anything unusual lands in your Review inbox with a short clip. Nothing is escalated until a person looks and confirms. No automatic accusations, ever.

3 · The right people act

Confirmed incidents notify the people you choose on their phones. Every confirm or dismiss improves accuracy, which you can see for your own store.

What you need

ComputerA PC that stays on. Seekur checks it during setup and recommends a plan it can actually run. An NVIDIA RTX 3060 or better unlocks every feature on ~16 cameras.
CamerasAny IP camera with RTSP (almost all), ideally ONVIF. 720p for queues; 1080p for theft and staff features.
PrivacyNo face recognition. Video stays on the store computer; only alerts and counts leave it. You set how long recordings are kept.
