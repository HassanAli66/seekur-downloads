How it works

It watches. You decide. The alarm does the rest.

01 · WATCH

Watches every camera

Seekur runs on a computer in your store and reads each camera's video, all day.

02 · SPOT

Spots hidden items

It follows body movement and bags, and flags a hand moving an item into a bag or clothing.

03 · ALERT

Sends you the clip

The clip lands on your phone, or on whoever you chose. Nobody is accused automatically.

04 · ACT

You confirm, the alarm sounds

Confirm with one tap to sound the alarm in the store. Dismiss it and nothing happens.

How we measured it

  • 177 video clips of staged shoplifting and normal shopping, from a public research dataset.
  • Every clip was judged by a model that had never seen it (5-fold cross-validation), so memorising can't flatter the numbers.
  • Measured through the same detection code the store runs, safety checks included.

What it means for you

  • Treat alerts as a second pair of eyes, not a guarantee.
  • The clips were filmed in a room set up like a shop. Real stores are harder: crowds, shelves in the way, ceiling cameras.
  • These figures are for today's model. Every new model has to beat them before it reaches you.

Privacy

Smart, without being creepy.

Video stays in your store

Live video and recordings are analysed and kept on your own computer. Only the clips of alerts you confirm or dismiss are sent to the learning library.

No face recognition

Seekur reads body movement and bags, never faces. It doesn't know who anyone is, and doesn't try.

A person decides

Every alert is a "please check this". Nothing is escalated and no alarm sounds until someone confirms.

Questions

Good to know.

Will it catch every shoplifter?

No camera system does. On staged footage, today's model caught about half of concealments, and 93% of its alerts were real. Each new model has to do better before it's released.

Does video leave my store?

Live video and recordings stay on your computer. Only clips of alerts you confirm or dismiss go to the Seekur learning library, which you agree to when you sign up. Alerts also go to the phones you choose.

Do I need new cameras?

Usually not. Any IP camera that can stream over RTSP works, and almost all of them can.

Do I need to install a Seekur app on my phone?

No. Alerts come through apps like ntfy or Telegram, which are free. Seekur shows you how to connect each one.

What happens when Seekur gets better?

Your app downloads the new model in the background and switches to it without a restart. You'll see which version is running in Settings.

When can I download it?
