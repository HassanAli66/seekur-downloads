How it works

It watches. You decide. The right people act.

It watches

Open-source AI finds people, bags and body posture in each camera image, then checks them against rules you control: zones you draw, timers, opening hours.

You confirm

Anything unusual lands in your Review inbox with a snapshot and a short clip. Nothing is escalated until a person looks — no automatic accusations, ever.

The right people act

Confirmed incidents notify the people you choose, on their phones (free ntfy app or Telegram), with quiet hours and per-person rules. Every confirm or dismiss feeds your store's own accuracy report.

Clips: Shoplifting Dataset (2022), CV Laboratory MNNIT Allahabad (Ansari & Singh), doi:10.17632/r3yjf35hzr.1, licensed CC BY 4.0. Edited by Seekur: faces blurred, AI overlay and labels added. Staged scenes recorded in a lab.

How we measured it

  • 177 real video clips of staged shoplifting and normal shopping, from a public research dataset (CC BY 4.0).
  • Every clip was judged by a model that had never seen it (5-fold cross-validation), so the numbers aren't flattered by memorising.
  • Measured through the exact code your store runs, safety checks included, not a lab-only shortcut.
  • A built-in safety check: the model may only alert when a hand has just moved to the body or into a bag, so a shopper standing with hands in pockets is never flagged.

What this means for you

  • Think of concealment alerts as a second pair of eyes, not a guarantee: they catch about half of clear cases, with very few false alarms.
  • The footage was recorded in a lab set up like a shop. Real stores are harder: crowds, shelves in the way, ceiling cameras.
  • Seekur includes Test mode and a clip recorder, so you can measure it on your own cameras before relying on it.
  • The other behaviours (restricted areas, after-hours, queues, lingering) are simpler and very reliable.

Questions

Good to know

Will it catch every shoplifter?

No camera system does. On real staged footage Seekur flagged about half of concealments, and 93% of its alerts were real. It's a second pair of eyes that rarely wastes your staff's time, and you can measure it on your own cameras with Test mode.

Does video go to the cloud?

No. Analysis runs on the store's computer and recordings stay there. Only alert titles, counts and your subscription status are sent, plus alert snapshots to the phones you choose. Exported clips have faces blurred.

What if the internet goes down?

Seekur keeps detecting and alerting on the store network, and works for 30 days without reaching the licence server. Everything syncs when the connection is back: alerts that couldn't be sent are delivered, and the stores overview catches up.

Do I need new cameras?

Usually not. Any IP camera with RTSP works, and most support ONVIF so Seekur can find them automatically. 1080p is recommended for concealment.

Can I download for a different computer?

Yes. Every version is in the Download section, whatever device you're browsing on.

Can staff use it on their phones?

Yes. Open the dashboard on a phone and choose “Add to Home Screen”: it works like an app. For alerts that ring even when the phone is locked, add the phone in Settings → Notifications (free ntfy app). Turn on remote access to open the dashboard and alerts away from the store's Wi-Fi.
